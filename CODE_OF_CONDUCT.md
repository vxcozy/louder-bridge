# Code of conduct

Louder Bridge should be a place where people can work together without
harassment or intimidation.

Be respectful, assume good intent, and focus criticism on the work. Do not use
sexualized language or imagery, make personal attacks, share someone else's
private information, or discriminate based on identity, background, ability,
or experience.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made
without conflicts of interest.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security contact. Reports will be handled discreetly. Do not use a public issue
to report harassment or disclose personal information.
